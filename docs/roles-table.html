Roles and permissions

Millennia Village field data capture 17 November 2026 3 roles

Field worker

Captures records on the phone and finishes the remediation work a supervisor assigns to them. Sees the whole site read-only.

Supervisor

Reviews submissions, assigns and verifies remediation work, keeps the monitoring zones, and exports reports.

Admin

Everything a supervisor can do, plus deleting records, managing accounts and roles, and reading the activity log.

Where capture happens. Records are created in the mobile app. Supervisors and admins can sign in there with the same account, so the web dashboard is a review and reporting surface rather than a capture form.

Deleting. An admin may delete any record. Everyone else may withdraw their own, and only while it is still pending — once reviewed, it stays.

Fixes. The job holder is the assigned worker, or the reporter when nobody was assigned. Only a supervisor or admin can verify the fix and close it, and closing it is what moves the Village Health Score.
